Put your link-preview image here, named:   og-image.jpg

Size: 1200 x 630 pixels. This is what LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Slack, Facebook
and X show when someone shares your portfolio link. Without it they show a
plain text card with no picture.

Easiest way to make one:
  1. Open the site, walk to a view you like (the desk with the lamp on, or
     the bookshelf with the TV, both photograph well).
  2. Take a screenshot.
  3. Crop it to 1200 x 630 and save it here as og-image.jpg.

Then open index.html and replace https://example.com with your real
domain in the canonical / og:url / og:image / twitter:image tags — those
must be absolute URLs or the preview is ignored.
